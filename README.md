# 🐾 Virtual Pet Simulator 🐾

Welcome to your very own Digital Pet Playground, where cuteness meets code! This Python-based pet emulator lets you care for, play with, and train a virtual buddy — and it's packed with personality. Whether you’re raising a cat, dog, bird, or even a turtle, this project brings your console to life 🐶🐱🐦🐢.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To build an interactive terminal-based simulator using OOP concepts in Python. Users can create a pet and interact with it through various actions like feeding, playing, sleeping, and even teaching tricks!

---

## 🧠 Features & Fun Facts

| Feature | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 🐶 `name` | Every pet needs a name! Choose one or get a fun random suggestion. |
| 🍚 `hunger` | Tracks how hungry your pet is (0 = full, 10 = very hungry). |
| ⚡ `energy` | Determines how active your pet is (0 = exhausted, 10 = fully rested). |
| 🐱 `happiness` | The mood lifter — the higher, the better! |
| 🎓 `experience` | Pets level up as they learn tricks and play games. |
| 🎂 `age` | Your pet grows over time (especially while sleeping). |
| 🧠 `mood` | A dynamic attribute calculated from energy and happiness. |
| 🎃 `tricks` | Teach your pet new tricks and see their progress! |

---

## 🕹️ How It Works

Create your pet and interact through a simple terminal menu:

1. 🍖 Feed your pet
2. ⚽ Play with your pet
3. ✍️  Teach your pet a trick
4. 📃 Check your pet's status
5. 😴 Let your pet sleep
6. 🎃 Show known tricks
7. 🎮 Play a mini-game
8. ❌ Exit
